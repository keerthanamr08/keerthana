IoT  /  Mechatronics

Automatic Cloth Folding Machine

A compact robot / mechanism that can detect, grip, and fold garments automatically — inspired by commercial robots like Foldimate.

The Automatic Cloth Folding Machine is a mechatronics innovation that automates the labor-intensive task of folding laundry items like t-shirts, towels, and pants.

It uses sensors, motors, and programmed flaps to detect garment size and execute precise creases in under 30 seconds per item. Built with Arduino, wooden platforms, DC servos, and IR sensors for cloth detection — it grips, rotates, and stacks clothes neatly, achieving high success rates on varied fabrics.

The project showcases skills in electronics, embedded programming, and mechanical design while addressing real-world efficiency needs in homes and laundromats.
